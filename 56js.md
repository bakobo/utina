# Revocation is unresolved: endorsement ACDCs carry no registry, so nothing can be revoked
kind: idea
tags: dossier
created: 2026-08-15T02:21Z

- 2026-08-15T02:21Z Ruled 2026-08-15 for tick 2coc: endorsement ACDCs are built with NO registry, so they are structurally unrevokable, and whether revocation should EVER enter the domain's vocabulary is deliberately unresolved. Context: the constructor commits no revocation operator by design — a party who changes their mind declines, which is another issuance (src/utina/enact/constructor.py ISSUANCE comment), and @tdsrgi builds the conflict rule on that. A TEL registry would make revocation representable; @65buz7 measured its cost on keripy 2.0.0-dev6 (v1 registry pinned inside a v2 KEL, a three-step anchor dance through the transaction-event verifier, two more nondeterminism knobs). Open questions when this is picked up: does a revoked endorsement read as SPENT (like a declination) or as RESTORED-to-pending, and which reading does the dossier spec's slot predicate require; does revocation deserve a demo beat; does @tdsrgi's decisive-declination rule survive contact with revocation. DECIDE WHEN: a scenario needs an endorsement withdrawn rather than declined, or dossier conformance is judged to require live revocation state.
- 2026-08-15T02:25Z Evidence from the dossier spec (read 2026-08-15): registry-less is CONFORMANT, not a shortcut. The Endorsed slot predicate is 'signed Endorsement ACDC with disp endorse and act appropriate to the operation, issued by the expected endorser and anchored in that endorser's KEL' (dossier-spec-body.md:223, :359) — revocation status is not a term in it, the normative endorsement schema (schemas/endorsement.json, SAID EAfn0gRMUnp6d1hyE5qJCN86kBFBp80JwMdm0BqiC1B0) has no registry field, and 'registry' appears once in the whole normative text (:228, the general graph-traversal step, disjunctive 'KELs or status registries'). Revocation in the joint-issuance section is modeled at the OPERATOR layer — RMxN/RMxQ revocation thresholds whose endorsements carry act 'revoke' (:370, :372) — not as a TEL on the endorsement. So if revocation ever enters utina's vocabulary, the dossier-shaped door is a revocation OPERATOR in the law, not a registry on the credential.
